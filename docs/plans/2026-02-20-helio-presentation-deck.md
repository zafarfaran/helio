## Slide 2 — The Problem

### Tax planning today is slow, manual, and risky.

- **45-90 minutes per client** — pulling data from HMRC portals, SA302s, P60s, pasting into spreadsheets, hoping the formulas are right
- **200 clients = 200+ hours/year** on tax analysis alone — that's five working weeks, gone
- **60% effective marginal rate** between £100k–£125,140 from the PA taper — one missed pension top-up costs clients thousands per year
- **FCA Consumer Duty** now requires advisers to evidence good outcomes — every missed optimisation is money lost *and* compliance risk
- **Planning is reactive** — opportunities caught at annual reviews, or not at all

## Slide 4 — What is Helio

### A conversational tax planning platform for UK financial advisers.

- **Instant tax position** — full UK tax computation in seconds: income tax bands, NI, Personal Allowance, Adjusted Net Income, HICBC, pension annual allowance
- **Scenario modelling** — salary sacrifice, pension contributions, SIPP top-ups — instant before/after with exact savings to the penny
- **Proactive observations** — the engine auto-flags PA taper triggers, HICBC exposure, unused pension headroom, expiring allowances — before you ask
- **Natural language** — ask in plain English, get structured answers backed by real computation
- **Browser extension** — sits on top of HMRC, Companies House, Xero, HL — one-click "Analyse in Helio"

*"Helio covers the full UK tax landscape — income tax, NI, dividends, capital gains, HICBC, marriage allowance, student loans, salary sacrifice, pension planning, and more. What you'll see in the demo is one scenario, but the engine handles the entire tax position end to end."*
